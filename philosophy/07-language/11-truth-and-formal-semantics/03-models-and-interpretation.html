Models and Interpretation

A model-theoretic interpretation assigns a domain and semantic values to symbols. Once assigned, recursive clauses determine truth and consequence. Natural-language interpretation asks a prior question: why do actual words have these values rather than others?

Formal semantics and linguistic metasemantics therefore divide labour. Model theory provides exact representations; causal history, convention, use, intention, and rational interpretation connect those representations to a language.

Formal structures

An L-structure:

M=⟨M,I⟩

contains a nonempty domain M and interpretation function I assigning:

  • constants to elements;
  • function symbols to functions;
  • predicates to relations.

Satisfaction:

M⊨φ

is defined recursively.

Model Theory studies structures and theories in their own right.

Interpretation as assignment

In formal semantics, an interpretation is stipulated mathematically. Multiple interpretations are possible; the language is not intrinsically tied to one.

For an artificial formal system, stipulation may be sufficient. For English, speakers inherit historical, social, and environmental practices not chosen by a semanticist.

The formal assignment represents those facts; it does not create them.

Intended interpretation

A theory can have many models. Speakers nevertheless intend arithmetic terms to concern natural numbers and "water" to concern water.

An intended model is selected through factors outside bare satisfaction:

  • ostension;
  • causal contact;
  • inferential role;
  • convention;
  • expert deference;
  • practical success;
  • interpretive charity.

No one factor works for every vocabulary.

Permutation arguments

If domain elements are permuted and predicate extensions transformed accordingly, every sentence can preserve its truth value:

I′(P)={π(x):x∈I(P)}.

Formal satisfaction alone cannot distinguish the original from the permuted interpretation.

This motivates Reference Indeterminacy. A metasemantics must constrain eligible assignments.

Model theory is not linguistic meaning

Model theory answers:

Given M, what satisfies the formulas?

Philosophy of language asks:

In virtue of what is M the right representation of this linguistic practice?

The first is mathematical; the second involves speakers and world. Conflating them makes interpretation appear exogenously fixed.

Scientific models

"Model" in science often means a simplified representation. A logical model is instead a structure satisfying a theory.

A scientific model can itself be formalized as a structure, but approximation, idealization, and representation introduce relations beyond satisfaction.

The distinction prevents conclusions about formal models from being transferred uncritically to scientific representation.

Internal and external perspectives

Inside a model, quantifiers range over its domain. From the metalanguage, theorists compare domains and interpretations.

Skolem-style results show that properties such as countability can differ between internal and external viewpoints without contradiction.

This relativity does not mean ordinary truth is arbitrary. It shows that semantic claims are made from an interpreted metalanguage whose role must be acknowledged.

Formal semantics for natural language

Natural-language models add:

  • worlds and times;
  • contexts;
  • semantic types;
  • discourse states;
  • events;
  • degrees;
  • alternatives.

These are modelling resources selected to explain linguistic patterns. Their usefulness does not automatically entail that each is a fundamental entity mentally represented by speakers.

Formal values can be instrumental, representational, or realist depending on the broader theory.

Assessment

Formal semantics requires interpretation but does not generate it from syntax alone. Its strength is conditional precision:

given values and rules⟹truth, entailment, and composition.

The condition is not a defect. It marks the interface where language history, cognition, practice, and reality enter. A complete account must expose rather than hide that interface.
